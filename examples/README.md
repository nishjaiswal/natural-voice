# Examples

Everything in this folder is made up. "Kit", the designer, and "Parcelpoint", the delivery app, are fictional, and so is every number. The outputs were written by hand, following the skill's rules, to show the kind of result to expect. They are illustrations, not recorded runs.

1. [`kit.voice.md`](kit.voice.md): a voice file after setup, with a core voice and three modes (case study, LinkedIn, email). It shows the shape of what Natural Voice learns about someone.
2. [`polish.md`](polish.md): an AI-written LinkedIn post, polished into Kit's voice, with the "show changes" summary underneath.
3. [`dictation.md`](dictation.md): what Kit said out loud about one screenshot, word for word, plus the read-back and questions Natural Voice came back with.
4. [`section.md`](section.md): the case study section it wrote from that dictation, in Kit's voice, with one `[ADD: ...]` gap where Kit didn't give a number.
