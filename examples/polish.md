# Polish: an AI draft rewritten in Kit's voice (example, fictional)

Kit asked an AI to "write a LinkedIn post about the delivery photo change" and pasted the result into Natural Voice with **polish**. Kit's voice file has *Show changes: summary*.

## What Kit pasted

> Proof of delivery isn't just a checkbox, it's a moment of trust. This matters. When we looked closely, we found that drivers were skipping photos at an alarming rate, rather than simply following the flow. By thoughtfully reimagining the experience and prioritizing the photo step, we created a seamless, dependable process that can help provide customers with greater peace of mind. Looking ahead, this adds another layer to how we think about delivery. The result? Skipped photos dropped to 9%.

## What Natural Voice gave back

> Drivers were skipping the delivery photo.
>
> We made the photo step the priority in the flow, and skipped photos dropped to 9%.
>
> [ADD: what customers use the photo for, if you want the post to say why it matters]

**What I changed**
- Removed 4 phrases that announced importance instead of saying what happened: "isn't just a checkbox, it's a moment of trust", "This matters", "Looking ahead, this adds another layer to how we think about delivery", "The result?"
- Removed 1 contrast with a claim nobody made: "rather than simply following the flow"
- Cut "seamless, dependable process" (your rules ban "seamless") and "thoughtfully reimagining": I said what was actually done instead
- Cut "can help provide customers with greater peace of mind" and "at an alarming rate": the draft gave no evidence for either, so I left them out rather than guess
- Rebuilt it in your LinkedIn shape: plain claim first, then what you did, then the number

**Kept on purpose**
- "We", as in the draft

**Hidden characters**
- Replaced 2 narrow no-break spaces with normal spaces

**Check these**
- If you know the skipped-photo rate before the change, add it ("dropped from X% to 9%").
- The post doesn't say why the photo matters to customers. Tell me and I'll add it in your words.

## Why it's this short

Natural Voice only uses facts that are in the text you give it. The AI draft had one real fact (skipped photos dropped to 9% after the photo step got priority) wrapped in a lot of padding. In Write mode, where Kit talks it through first, the same post would include the details Kit gives, like the parcel in one hand and the high-value signature rule.
