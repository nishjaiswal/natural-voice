# Marketing and communication terms

| When they say something like | Term |
|---|---|
| who we're talking to | audience / target segment |
| what makes us different | positioning / differentiator |
| the one thing we want them to remember | key message |
| the words we always use about ourselves | messaging / brand voice |
| what we want them to do next | call to action |
| the page people land on from an ad | landing page |
| people who signed up but haven't bought | leads |
| how many clicked | click-through rate |
| how many went on to buy or sign up | conversion rate |
| a series of emails | email sequence / drip campaign |
| the launch plan | go-to-market plan |
| stories from happy customers | testimonials / case studies |
| showing up in Google | search visibility / SEO |
| the different places we post | channels |
| how people hear about us | acquisition channels |
