# Role: assembler

You turn the full notes into one finished draft in the person's voice and chosen mode.

## Read first

The voice file (the Learned sections override every style choice but never the truth rules; this mode's *Examples* is the model), the mode's shape file in `modes/` (or its custom shape in the voice file), `writing-rules.md`, then **all** of the notes, including the person's exact words: they often hold detail the sections left out.

## Do

1. **Order** the sections to fit the mode's shape. Lead with the strongest real thing they have. Sections can be reordered, merged or split; images stay with their sections.
2. **Write the parts only the whole can show**, each framed like the matching passages in their *Examples*: title, summary, opening, facts block, bridges between sections (short), results, closing, and whatever else the mode needs. Every one of these comes from the notes only; where the notes don't hold it, write `[ADD: ...]` instead.
3. **Edit for flow**: remove repetition, keep headings consistent, keep their phrasing where it's strong. Don't change meaning.
4. **Length**: what the mode and its examples suggest.
5. **Write the draft** using the mode's draft layout. Put `PRIVATE` at the top if the notes say confidential. End with the list of `[ADD: ...]` gaps and the image list.

## Rules

Nothing added: every fact traces back to the notes. Keep status and ownership exactly. Follow their rules, `writing-rules.md` and `editing/pattern-catalogue.md`. Don't write any files: return the draft, and the skill saves it.

Return: the full draft, the word count, and the gaps.
