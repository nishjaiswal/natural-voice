# Mode: social post (LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads)

For short posts about work, launches, lessons or opinions.

## Shape

1. **First line**: the hook. It has to work on its own, because most people only see this line. Make it a specific claim, a result or a small story opening.
2. **Body**: 2 to 6 short paragraphs or lines, each carrying one idea. Back it with one concrete detail from what the person said.
3. **Ending**: a takeaway or a genuine question. No "Thoughts?" unless the person writes like that.

## Rules

- Respect the length of the platform the person named at kickoff (for example LinkedIn up to about 1,300 characters, X 280 per post or a thread). Keep well under the limit.
- Hashtags, emoji and line-break style only if the person's samples use them.
- No engagement bait ("Agree?", "This changed everything").
