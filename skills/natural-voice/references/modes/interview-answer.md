# Mode: interview answer

For answers the person will **say out loud** in an interview: behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time..."), portfolio walkthroughs and "why this decision" questions.

## Shape

Use situation, task, action, result, but keep it natural, not a recited template:
1. **Situation** (1 to 2 sentences): where, when, what was at stake.
2. **Task** (1 sentence): what the person was responsible for. If they didn't say, write `[ADD: what you were responsible for]`; never infer it.
3. **Action** (3 to 5 sentences): what *they* did and why. This is the heart of the answer. Prefer "I" for the parts they confirmed were theirs; keep "we" wherever they said "we".
4. **Result** (1 to 2 sentences): what happened, with a number if there is one and where it came from.
5. **Reflection** (optional, 1 sentence): what they would repeat or do differently.

## Rules

- 60 to 150 words for a short answer, up to 250 for a walkthrough. Mark a natural pause point if it runs long.
- It must be easy to say. Use short sentences, contractions, and no parentheses or semicolons.
- Use no jargon the person wouldn't say out loud.
- Offer a one-line version at the end for when the interviewer wants it shorter.

## Draft layout

```markdown
**Question:** <the question, if known>

<the answer as it would be spoken>

**Short version:** <one or two sentences>
**If they ask a follow-up:** <one likely follow-up and a one-line answer, using only facts given>

Gaps: <every [ADD: ...]>
```
