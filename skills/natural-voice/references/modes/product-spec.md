# Mode: product spec or one-pager

For product requirement docs, feature briefs, design rationale and decision records.

## Shape

1. **Title and one-line summary**: what is being built, and for whom.
2. **Problem**: who has it, how we know (evidence as stated), and, if they said, what happens if we do nothing.
3. **Goal and success measures**: what "working" looks like. Use `[ADD: ...]` if no measure was given.
4. **Proposal**: what will be built, in plain words, with images or flows where provided.
5. **Decisions and trade-offs**: each decision, the options considered (only those mentioned) and why this one was chosen.
6. **Out of scope**: what this deliberately does not do.
7. **Open questions**: everything uncertain, phrased as questions.
8. **Risks**: only those mentioned, or flagged as `[ADD: risks]`.

Tone: clear and neutral. Short paragraphs and lists are fine here. Keep the person's voice in the reasoning, not in decoration.

## Draft layout

```markdown
# <Title>
<One-line summary>

## Problem
## Goal and success measures
## Proposal
## Decisions and trade-offs
## Out of scope
## Open questions
## Risks

---
Gaps: <every [ADD: ...]>
```
