# Mode: blog post or article

For opinion pieces, how-tos, lessons learned and newsletter issues.

## Shape

1. **Title**: a specific claim or promise, not a topic. "Why our onboarding lost half its users" beats "Thoughts on onboarding".
2. **Opening** (1 short paragraph): the hook. This can be a scene, a surprising fact or a problem the reader has, but only one the person actually described; never an invented scene. Don't open with a dictionary definition or "In today's world".
3. **Body**: 3 to 6 sections, each with a heading that says something. Each section makes one point, backed by a concrete example from what the person said.
4. **Ending**: one concrete takeaway or a question the reader can act on. No summary of the summary.

Length: whatever the person's samples suggest. If there are no samples, aim for 600 to 1,200 words.

## Draft layout

```markdown
# <Title>
<Opening>

## <Heading that says something>
<Section>

...

<Ending>

---
Gaps: <every [ADD: ...]>
```
