## Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing

- **What it is:** a field guide to patterns in AI-generated text, written and maintained by volunteers in WikiProject AI Cleanup. [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing). We read [the revision of 8 October 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&oldid=1379229123).
- **How we used it:** as a source of patterns, dates and model differences for `references/editing/pattern-catalogue.md`. We describe every pattern in our own words and wrote our own examples. No sentences were copied. Where we repeat single words from its lists (for example the model word lists by era), we credit it next to them.

## Humanizer

- **What it is:** an MIT-licensed agent skill by Siqi Chen that rewrites AI-sounding text. [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer). We compared against version 3.1.0.
- **How we used it:** its grouping of patterns, its idea that some patterns count only in clusters ("weak alone"), some of its group names (for example "Inflation and borrowed authority", "Formatting by rule" and "Writing for the wrong reader"), and about ten of its pattern headings, reused word for word (for example "One-line closers and dramatic fragments", "Arguing with no one" and "Re-explaining what the reader knows"). The catalogue cites Humanizer's pattern numbers (§1 to §26) so readers can compare. Our descriptions, examples and rules are our own. We also cite [Humanizer issue #229](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/issues/229) (19 August 2026), an informal study of rewrite quality and detection.
