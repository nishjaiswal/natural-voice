# Natural Voice

The Natural Voice skill is installed. Its instructions are in `skills/natural-voice/SKILL.md` inside this extension.

When the person wants to write something in their own voice, polish or humanise a draft, remove hidden characters, or set up or train their voice, activate the `natural-voice` skill and follow it. Don't load its reference files until the skill tells you to.

If you are working on this repository itself rather than using the skill, follow `AGENTS.md`.
